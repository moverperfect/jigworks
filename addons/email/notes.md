The app sends email through Resend, an email service, with `sendEmail` from `src/server/email.ts` and the Secret `RESEND_API_KEY`. Use it for any email the app sends, rather than code of your own.

- **Sending:** `const sent = await sendEmail(c.env, { to, subject, text });`, with `html` for an email that should look better than plain text, `replyTo` for where replies go, and `from` to send from another address. It never throws: `sent` is `{ ok: true, id }`, or `{ ok: false, error }` with the reason in words. Send after the app has kept what matters, so a failed email loses nothing, and `console.error(sent.error)` when it fails. Tell the person using the app only when the email was for them, such as a confirmation.
- **HTML:** put what people typed into an email's `html` with `html` from `hono/html`, such as ``html`<p>${name} signed up.</p>`.toString()``, which escapes it. `text` needs no escaping.
- **Resend's test sender:** emails come from `sender` in `email.ts`, `onboarding@resend.dev`, which works with no setup but sends only to the address the Developer signed up to Resend with. Resend refuses any other address, and `error` says so. To send to anyone else, the Developer verifies a domain they own at resend.com/domains, by adding the records Resend gives to its DNS, and `sender` changes to an address at that domain: ask them for it with `ask`.
- **Addresses aren't Secrets.** Ask for an address, such as the one emails go to, with `ask`, never `ask_for_secret`. Keep the address emails go to in `wrangler.jsonc`'s `vars`, such as `"OWNER_EMAIL": "..."`, with its name in `__BaseEnv_Env`. The address they come from is `sender`.
- **Telling the Developer:** say the app uses Resend, an email service, and that it needs their key, with `ask_for_secret` once the app works. Say that until they set up their own domain with Resend, the app can only email the address they signed up to Resend with, so the owner's emails should go there; and that emailing anyone else, such as the people who sign up, needs a domain of their own, which Resend walks them through.
- **Tests** have no network, so replace `fetch` with `vi.spyOn(globalThis, "fetch").mockResolvedValue(Response.json({ id: "email-1" }))`, pass `{ RESEND_API_KEY: "a key" }` in the test's `env`, and read what was sent from the spy's calls, as `src/server/email.test.ts` does.
