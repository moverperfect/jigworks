A page keeps up to date with what other people change, such as a sign-up list showing the names others add, with `poll` from `src/client/live.ts`. Apps can't hold a connection open to the Worker, so the page asks one of the app's routes every few seconds instead. Use it for any part of a page that should change without being reloaded, rather than a timer of your own.

- **The route** is an ordinary GET route under `/api/`, answering the whole of what the page shows from storage, such as `app.get("/api/signups", etag(), async (c) => c.json(await signups.list()))`. `etag()`, from `hono/etag`, gives the answer an ETag, and answers 304 without the body when the page already has it, so an ask with nothing new sends back next to nothing, though the route still reads what it answers each time. Give each part of the page that changes on its own its own route, such as a count apart from a long list, so a change to one doesn't send the other again.
- **The page** starts asking in a `useEffect`, which stops it when the component goes: `useEffect(() => poll<Signup[]>("/api/signups", setSignups), []);`, with the answer's type in `src/shared/api.ts`, which both sides import. `poll` asks at once, so the page needs no fetch of its own to start, and then calls its function only when the answer has changed. Show something such as "Loading…" until the first answer.
- **How often:** every 5 seconds, then more slowly while nothing changes, up to every 30 seconds, and every 5 again after a change. `poll(url, onChange, { every, slowest })` changes those, in milliseconds. Keep to the defaults unless the Developer asks for something quicker: asking more often than every 2 seconds costs more without looking any quicker.
- **A hidden page doesn't ask,** such as one in a background tab or on a locked phone, and asks at once when it's shown again. This, and asking more slowly when nothing changes, keeps the app's requests, and so its costs, low however long people leave it open.
- **A change the page makes itself,** such as someone adding their name, shows from that change's own answer at once, as it would without this add-on: the next ask brings everyone else's.
- **An answer that isn't OK,** or none, such as when the phone is offline, is skipped: the page keeps what it showed and asks again later.
- **Tests** have no page and no network. `src/client/live.test.ts` shows how: `vi.stubGlobal("document", ...)` for the page, `vi.spyOn(globalThis, "fetch")` for the route, and `vi.useFakeTimers()` with `vi.advanceTimersByTimeAsync` for the time between asks. Test a route itself as any other, with `app.request`: a second request with `If-None-Match` set to the first answer's `ETag` header gets 304.
