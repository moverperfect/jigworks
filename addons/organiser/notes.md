The app has an organiser's area at `/organiser`, whose code is in `src/server/organiser.ts`: a part of the app that only someone with the passcode, the Secret `ORGANISER_PASSCODE`, can open. Use it for anything only the person running the app should see or change, such as everyone's details or the rota.

- **Signing in:** `/organiser/sign-in` is the sign-in page. Link to it from wherever the organiser starts. Signing in goes back to the page that sent the organiser there, or else to `/organiser`, so give the area a page there: a page of the front end, or `organiser.get("/", ...)` in an app with no front end. Signing in lasts 30 days, in a cookie named `__Host-organiser`. Signing out is a form that posts to `/organiser/sign-out`: `<form method="post" action="/organiser/sign-out"><button>Sign out</button></form>`.
- **A route only the organiser can use** goes on `organiser`, before `app.route("/organiser", organiser)` in the Worker's main module: Hono copies the routes when it mounts them, so a route added after is never found. `organiser.get("/api/signups", ...)` answers `/organiser/api/signups`. For anyone who isn't signed in, a page there sends the browser to the sign-in page, which comes back to it after, and any other request gets a 401.
- **A page of the front end** for the organiser goes under `/organiser`, such as `/organiser/rota`, which the front end shows for `location.pathname`. The page itself isn't secret; its data is. Fetch it from the organiser's routes under `/organiser/api/`, and when one answers 401, send the browser to sign in: `location.assign("/organiser/sign-in?next=" + encodeURIComponent(location.pathname))`.
- **A page the Worker makes,** in an app with no front end, is a route on `organiser` that returns HTML.
- **Forms from other sites:** the platform refuses a change, such as a form's post, that another site or another app sends, so the area needs no CSRF token.
- **A wrong passcode** is refused, the same way however close it was. After 20 wrong passcodes within 15 minutes, from anyone, signing in pauses for up to 15 minutes. Changing the passcode signs everyone out. Once anyone has used the area with the new one, putting the old one back doesn't sign them in again.
- **The sign-in page** is `signInPage` in `organiser.ts`. Change its words or styles freely, but keep its form's fields.
- **Tests** pass the passcode in the test's `env`, with `STORE`: `{ STORE: fakeStore(), ORGANISER_PASSCODE: "a passcode" }`. To test an organiser's route, sign in with `app.request("/organiser/sign-in", { method: "POST", body: new URLSearchParams({ passcode: "a passcode" }) }, env)`, then send the `Set-Cookie` header's cookie back, as `src/server/organiser.test.ts` does.
